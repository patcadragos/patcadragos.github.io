Documenting how I build things from scratch, one project at a time.

About

I'm an engineering student who builds real projects from zero. My first project is a DIY air conditioning unit — no kit, no shortcuts, just figuring it out as I go.

I document the whole process, including the failures, and share it as short-form video content across platforms.

This site's content pipeline uses TikTok's Content Posting API to help manage and publish video content from my creative process to my own TikTok account. Login Kit is used to authenticate my TikTok account securely via OAuth.
